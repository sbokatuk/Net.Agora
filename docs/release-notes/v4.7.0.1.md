## What's changed

`Net.Agora.Video` / `.Maui` and `Net.Agora.Voice` / `.Maui` advance to **4.7.0.1**. The cross-platform
API is unchanged. Underneath, the RTC SDK moves to **4.7.0 on iOS and macOS** and **4.6.4 on Android**,
where 4.7.0 hasn't reached Maven yet.

| Platform | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android | 4.6.3 (`Net.Agora.*.Android` 4.6.3.5) | 4.6.4 ([4.6.4.1](https://github.com/sbokatuk/Net.Agora.Android/releases/tag/v4.6.4.1)) |
| iOS | 4.6.2 (`Net.Agora.*.iOS` 4.6.2.5) | 4.7.0 ([4.7.0.1](https://github.com/sbokatuk/Net.Agora.iOS/releases/tag/v4.7.0.1)) |
| macOS | 4.6.2 (`Net.Agora.*.Mac` 4.6.2.2) | 4.7.0 ([4.7.0.1](https://github.com/sbokatuk/Net.Agora.Mac/releases/tag/v4.7.0.1)) |

The version number follows the iOS line, as it always has. That is a fixed rule rather than a claim
that every platform runs 4.7.0: Android runs 4.6.4 in this release. See
[Version numbers](https://github.com/sbokatuk/Net.Agora/blob/main/docs/BUILD.md#version-numbers).

### With Signaling, take Signaling 2.2.6.4 too

RTC 4.7.0 ships `aosl` 1.3.16, and its engine imports five symbols that the 1.3.5 in earlier Signaling
packages lacks. An app keeps one `aosl.framework`, so an app with Video or Voice 4.7.0.1 and an older
`Net.Agora.Signaling` can fail to load the engine on iOS and macOS. Use
[`Net.Agora.Signaling` 2.2.6.4](https://github.com/sbokatuk/Net.Agora/releases/tag/signaling-v2.2.6.4)
or newer alongside this release. `Net.Agora.Chat` is safe at any current version.

### If you reference extension packages on iOS

From 4.7.0, eight of the twelve `Net.Agora.Extensions.*.iOS` packages link a library only the Video
package ships, so referencing one of them in a **Voice** app stops it in dyld before `main`. Only
`Ains`, `Aiaec`, `AudioBeauty` and `SpatialAudio` work with Voice. Video apps are unaffected, and so
is every Android and macOS app. `Net.Agora.Extensions.VideoEncoder` no longer carries
`video_enc.xcframework` on iOS or macOS: the codec moved inside the extension. Details are in the iOS
and macOS release notes linked above.

### iOS Voice apps can't end themselves with `Environment.Exit`

Once a Voice engine has existed, `exit(3)` never returns on iOS 4.7.0: an exit handler in Agora's
`AgoraffmpegExtension` spins. Apps that iOS terminates, which is how iOS apps normally end, aren't
affected, and neither is Video. If you do need to end the process, call `_exit` from libSystem.

### Samples

`Net.Agora.Sample.Voice.Mac` gained a Token field, so it can join a project with an App Certificate.

## Tested

- Package and unit tests, and the device suites for Video and Voice on `net8`, `net9` and `net10` on
  an Android emulator, an iOS simulator and the macOS host, plus Video with Signaling in one Android
  app.
- Live, in one channel with tokens signed for the run: the Video sample on Android and iOS (`net8`
  and `net9`) and `Net.Agora.Sample.Video.Mac` joined and saw each other; the Android camera rendered
  as remote video on iOS and on macOS, and the Mac camera rendered locally. Background blur, video
  enhancement and AI noise suppression each switched on and off with no error on both mobile
  platforms. The Voice samples on the same three platforms joined one channel: Android and iOS
  reported the Mac speaking by uid, iOS reported Android, and mute state passed both ways.
